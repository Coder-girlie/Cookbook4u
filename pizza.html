Pizza recipe :D

  • In a mixing bowl or pan, take all-purpose flour, salt, sugar, baking soda and baking powder.
  • Add 2 tablespoons olive oil and lemon juice.
  • Add water in parts. begin to mix and then knead the dough.
  • Keep on kneading till you get a soft and smooth dough. Add water in parts. I added ½ cup + 2 tablespoons water.
  • Knead at least for 10 to 12 minutes.
  • The dough should become elastic and stretchable. Take a portion and stretch it. The dough should not break.
  • Rub some olive oil all over the dough and keep covered in a bowl. Allow the dough to leaven for 2 to 2.30 hours.
  • Once leavened, punch one of your fingers in the dough.
  • Again knead the dough lightly before using it. If not using immediately, then cover the dough in an aluminium foil or keep it covered in a closed container in the refrigerator.
  • You can also freeze the dough and it will stay good for a month. Take the pizza dough and divide it into 5 equal parts.
  • Take one part and roll it well between your palms. Flatten and keep on the rolling board. Sprinkle some flour.
  • With a rolling pin, gently roll to a slightly thin round pizza. Roll to get a 0.3 to 0.5 cm thickness. Sprinkle some more flour if required while rolling.
  • Heat a pan and brush or spread some olive oil on it.
  • Now gently place the pizza base on the pan.
  • Cook the pizza base on simmer or low heat.
  • Flip the pizza base when you see bubbles appearing on it and the base has turned golden.
  • Now, quickly spread the pizza sauce on this cooked side of the pizza base.
  • Sprinkle grated pizza cheese or mozzarella cheese. You can add cheese as required.
  • Top with the marinated vegetables. While doing so, place the onions, tomatoes and bell peppers first and then drizzle a bit of the oil-juices mixture from the marinade all over.
  • Cover with a lid and let the pizza cook on simmer or low heat.
  • Cook the pizza till the base becomes golden and crisp and the cheese has melted.
  • Remove and serve Pizza hot with tomato ketchup. You can also sprinkle red chili flakes on top while serving!! byee :)
