Delicious cookiezz XD

Ingredients

  • Dry ingredients
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Wet ingredients and mix-ins
  • ¾ cup (1½ sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

  • Instructions

  • Prepare the oven and pans: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  • Combine dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set it aside.
  • Cream butter and sugar: In a large bowl, use a stand mixer or electric beaters to beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until the mixture is light and fluffy. This can take about 2–3 minutes.
  • Add eggs and vanilla: Beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract, until well-combined.
  • Add dry mixture to wet mixture: Gradually add the flour mixture into the wet ingredients. Mix on low speed just until the flour disappears into the dough. Do not overmix, cuz this can make the cookies tough.
  • Fold in chocolate chips: Use a spatula to gently fold in the chocolate chips until they are evenly distributed.
  • Scoop the dough: Drop rounded tablespoon-sized balls of dough onto your prepared baking sheets, spacing them about 2 inches apart.
  • Bake the cookies: Bake for 9 to 11 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown. The centers may still look underdone, but they will set as they cool.
  • Tips for perfect cookies
  • Room temperature ingredients: Use room temperature butter and eggs so they cream together more smoothly.
  • Prevent flat cookies: For thicker cookies that don't spread too much, chill the dough in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before baking.
  • Use parchment paper: Line your baking sheets with parchment paper to prevent the cookies from sticking
  • Don't overbake: For a soft, chewy center, pull the cookies out when the edges are set but the middle is still pale.
